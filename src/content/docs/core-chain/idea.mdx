<span class="stage-badge stage-idea">Idea</span>

An **Idea** is an unverified hunch or "what if." It's volatile by nature — most Ideas either get
promoted (turn out to be true or useful) or discarded (checked and found wrong, or not worth
pursuing).

- **Folder:** `<Domain>/Idea/`
- **File structure:** one file per idea
- **Nature:** unverified, volatile

## Status lifecycle

Unlike Knowledge, Decision, or Lesson, an Idea carries a `status` field in its frontmatter that
tracks where it is in its own life:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `open` | Still unverified, sitting there. |
| `promoted` | Turned out true/useful, became a [Knowledge](../knowledge/) or [Decision](../decision/) entry. Link to that entry so you can trace "this idea became that fact." |
| `discarded` | Checked it, it was wrong or not worth pursuing. **Keep the note anyway** — a wrong hunch is still useful data on your own instincts over time. |

```yaml
---
type: idea
status: open
date: 2026-10-01
---
```

:::note
Discarded doesn't mean deleted. The value of an Idea log isn't just the hits — it's the honest
record of what you guessed wrong, too.
:::
