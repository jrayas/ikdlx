<span class="stage-badge stage-decision">Decision</span>

A **Decision** is knowledge + context + reasoning → a conclusion. It's a specific question you
had, and how you answered it (e.g. "copper vs aluminium for this PCB"). Reusable across multiple
future assignments and courses, even though it started from one.

- **Folder:** `<Domain>/Decision/`
- **File structure:** one file per decision
- **Nature:** context-bound reasoning

## Shape of a Decision note

A Decision entry typically records:

1. The question you were actually trying to answer
2. The options considered
3. The reasoning that ruled options in or out
4. The conclusion you reached

```yaml
---
type: decision
course: micro-grid   # optional — only if tied to a specific course
date: 2026-10-01
---
```

## Decisions vs. Assignments

A Decision is **not** the deliverable itself — it's the reasoning behind one piece of it. An
[Assignment](../../supporting-types/#assignment) links out to the Decisions used to produce it rather
than duplicating the reasoning inline. This is what lets one Decision (e.g.
`pcb-copper-vs-aluminium`) get reused across several unrelated assignments later.
