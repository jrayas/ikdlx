---
title: Why not PARA
description: What PARA gets wrong for engineering coursework, and what IKDLx organises around instead.
---

PARA (**P**rojects, **A**reas, **R**esources, **A**rchives) organises by lifecycle and
ownership — active vs. reference vs. done. It works well for general life admin, but flattens
all reference material into one "Resources" bucket regardless of whether it's a settled fact,
a reasoned judgement call, or a quick-recall cheatsheet.

For engineering coursework and project work, that distinction matters: a fact and a hard-won
decision are not the same kind of thing and shouldn't be filed the same way.

IKDLx instead organises by **epistemic type** — what kind of thinking produced this note — and
keeps **domain** (Electrical, Electronics, Mechatronics, etc.) as the top-level container, so
everything about one domain is in one place.

:::tip[The core distinction]
PARA asks *"is this active, reference, or archived?"*
IKDLx asks *"how sure am I about this, and how did I get here?"*
:::
