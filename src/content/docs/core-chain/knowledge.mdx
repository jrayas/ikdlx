<span class="stage-badge stage-knowledge">Knowledge</span>

**Knowledge** is a confirmed, context-free fact. It's stable, rarely rewritten, and reusable
forever — it doesn't depend on the specific problem or course that surfaced it.

- **Folder:** `<Domain>/Knowledge/`
- **File structure:** one file per fact
- **Nature:** verified, stable

## What makes something Knowledge rather than a Decision

If the note is answering "what is true" independent of any specific situation, it's Knowledge.
The moment it becomes "what is true, *for this specific problem*, and why I chose it" — that's a
[Decision](/core-chain/decision/) instead.

```yaml
---
type: knowledge
date: 2026-10-01
---
```

An [Idea](/core-chain/idea/) that gets verified and turns out to be a general, reusable fact gets
promoted here — set that Idea's `status: promoted` and link to the resulting Knowledge note.
