---
title: Cross-domain notes
description: Where a concept that spans two domains should live, and how to handle deleting a domain.
---

When a concept genuinely spans two domains (e.g. STATCOM control theory touching both Electrical
and Control Systems), it lives in whichever domain owns it primarily. Other domains reference it
via a normal wikilink rather than duplicating the content.

:::caution[Before deleting a domain folder]
Check its backlinks first, since other domains may silently depend on notes inside it. Prefer
**renaming** to `_Archive/<DomainName>/` over outright deletion, so a broken cross-domain link is
a two-step, reversible mistake rather than a one-click permanent one.
:::
